# Community conduct

Discuss technical ideas respectfully. Explain disagreements with reproducible
examples and sources. Welcome contributions from people of different backgrounds
and experience levels. Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks and
publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may moderate content or restrict participation to protect constructive
collaboration. Once the repository is published, contact its listed maintainers
through their available private GitHub contact channels for conduct concerns.
